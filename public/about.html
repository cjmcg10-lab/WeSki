Your mountain. Your crew.

A 3D map of the ski resort with your friends on it. See where everyone is, meet up, and get directions along runs at your level.

Open SkiWithMe

Works in your phone’s browser. No download needed; add it to your home screen in two taps.

What it does

3D mountain map

Every run, lift and lodge on a 3D model of the mountain built from laser-scanned terrain. The map you open stays on your phone that day, even without signal.

Ski with friends

Create a private group, share the code, and see your friends on the map when they choose to share their location.

Meet up

Pick a meeting point everyone can reach, and get directions there or to any friend in your group.

Directions at your level

With the pass, turn-by-turn and spoken directions anywhere on the mountain: to any run, lift, lodge or dropped pin, avoiding runs above the level you choose.

Your ski day

Runs, lifts, vertical, speed and distance for the day. Every day you track is saved on your phone; the pass opens them all, with replays.

Checked ratings

Run ratings are checked against each resort’s own trail map, so a run is shown at the level the resort gives it.

Add it to your phone

iPhone:

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Tap “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add”.

Android:

  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”.

Safety

SkiWithMe is a guide, not a safety device. Maps, ratings and directions can be wrong or out of date, and GPS can be off by tens of metres. Resort signs, closures and ski patrol always come first. Please read the Terms of Use before skiing with it.

Your privacy

Your location stays on your phone unless you choose to share it with a group. Your ski days are saved on your phone, not on our servers. We do not sell personal information or show ads. Read the Privacy Policy.
