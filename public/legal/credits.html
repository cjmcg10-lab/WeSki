Map and data credits

Last updated 22 November 2026

SkiWithMe is built on open data. Thank you to everyone who makes it.

Trails, lifts, buildings and places

© OpenStreetMap contributors. OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL). SkiWithMe’s map of each mountain, including its directions data, is a database made from OpenStreetMap data with corrections (positions checked against aerial photos, ratings from the resorts’ published trail lists, runs traced where missing). Under the ODbL, that database is available on request: write to the contact address below and we will send it, under the same licence.

Worldwide resort list

The Forecast’s list of ski resorts (names, places, lift and run positions and heights) is made from OpenSkiData / OpenSkiMap.org, © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), with ski area records from Skimap.org and place names from Who’s On First. Heights that looked wrong were checked against the Copernicus GLO-30 elevation model (© DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA, all rights reserved; free for use including commercial use). Time zones come from timezonefinder (MIT) and its ODbL boundary data. The resort list we publish is a derived database under the ODbL and is available on request, like the map database above.

Pin map

When you add a mountain by dropping a pin, the map picture comes from OpenFreeMap, © OpenMapTiles, data from © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), drawn with MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3-Clause). The height of a pin is read from the AWS Terrain Tiles listed under Terrain below.

Ratings

Run ratings are checked against each resort’s own published trail list or trail map. Trail names and ratings are facts published by the resorts; resort names belong to their owners. SkiWithMe is not affiliated with or endorsed by any resort.

Trees

  • Where the trees stand and how tall: High Resolution Canopy Height Maps by Meta and World Resources Institute (WRI), 2024, Registry of Open Data on AWS, licensed CC BY 4.0. We summarise it per resort (tree cover and average height on a 7-13 m grid) to place the trees on the map.

Weather

Weather in the USA: the US National Weather Service (public domain). Weather elsewhere: data from MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute), licensed under CC BY 4.0. We change it to fit the app: snowfall outside the USA is worked out from precipitation and temperature, wind gusts are estimated where MET Norway gives none, and summit and base values are adjusted to their real heights.

Weather alerts (winter storm, snow, ice, high wind and wind chill) in the USA: US National Weather Service (public domain). In Canada: Data Source: Environment and Climate Change Canada, under the Data Services End-use Licence; the name of each alert is shown as issued. In Norway: alerts from MET Norway (MetAlerts), licensed under CC BY 4.0. Alerts only add a note to a day: they never change its score.
