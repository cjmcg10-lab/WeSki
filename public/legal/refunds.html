Refund Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Passes are sold by our reseller Paddle, which handles payments and refunds. To ask for a refund, use the link in your Paddle receipt email, or contact Paddle through paddle.net. You can also write to us at hello@builtbyroger.app and we will pass it on.

Season Pass and Crew Season Pass

  • Full refund within 14 days of buying or of a yearly renewal, no questions asked.
  • Cancel any time to stop the next renewal; the pass keeps working until the end of the year already paid.

Day Pass and Week Pass

  • Full refund within 14 days of buying if you have not used the pass at a mountain yet.
  • If something in the app did not work as described for you while the pass was running (for example directions would not load), tell us within 14 days and we will refund it.

Your legal rights

In the European Union and the United Kingdom you have a 14-day right to withdraw from a purchase of digital services. When you start using a pass straight away, you ask us to begin the service within those 14 days and accept that, once a Day or Week Pass has been fully used, the right to withdraw ends; for a pass you have partly used, a refund may be reduced in proportion to the time used. This policy does not take away any right you have under the law where you live.

Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 working days. A refunded pass ends when the refund is made.
