Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

In short. Your location is the most sensitive thing SkiWithMe handles, so it stays on your phone unless you choose to share it. Your ski days are saved on your phone, not on our servers. Friends see your position only while you switch sharing on in a group. If you switch on “Help improve the maps”, the app sends a summary of each ski day with no track, no times and nothing that says who you are, and we only use places that at least five different ski days reached. We do not sell or rent personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not share data for advertising.

Who we are

SkiWithMe is run by Built By Roger LLC (“we”, “us”), which decides how the personal information described here is used (the “controller”). Contact us about privacy at hello@builtbyroger.app.

What stays only on your phone

Your saved ski days and their GPS tracks, settings, favourite mountains, closures you mark, your display name and rider, and the downloaded maps. Removing the app or clearing its website data deletes them. We cannot see or recover them. Saved ski days are never deleted by us: without a pass they are listed as locked, and a pass opens them all. Without a pass, the map of a mountain is kept for the day you open it and removed from the phone the next time the app opens online on a later day; downloads made with a pass stay while the pass lasts. The phone keeps a short list of which mountains you opened today and which you downloaded, for this.

Who else receives data

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and our servers.
  • Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd) is the reseller that sells the passes. When you buy, you give your payment details to Paddle, under Paddle’s privacy policy. Paddle tells us that a purchase was made, for which plan and account.
  • Our email provider sends the sign-in emails to your address.
  • Weather: your phone asks our server for the forecast at the mountain’s summit and base, and our server asks the US National Weather Service or MET Norway. Only the mountain’s position is sent, not yours.

We do not sell or share personal information for money or for advertising, and we do not use it to profile you. We would disclose information only if the law requires it, and then only what is required.

Your choices and rights

  • Location: your phone asks before SkiWithMe can use it, and you can turn it off in the phone’s settings at any time.
  • Group sharing: off until you switch it on, and you can pause it at any moment.
  • Map help: off until you switch it on (More → Privacy & map help). Switching it off stops it at once. Days skied before you switched it on are never sent.
  • Ski-day feedback: you choose each time; tap Skip to send nothing. An answer carries no identity, so it cannot be looked up or deleted afterwards.
  • Delete your account in the app at any time. You can also ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, by writing to the address below. We answer within 30 days.
  • If we decline a request, we will tell you why, and you can appeal by replying to that email with “Appeal” in the subject. We answer appeals within 60 days. If you are still not satisfied, you can contact your state’s Attorney General (in Connecticut: portal.ct.gov/ag).
  • Depending on where you live (for example the European Union, the United Kingdom, California and several other US states), you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, to object to or limit some uses, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Precise location is “sensitive data” under the Connecticut Data Privacy Act and similar laws: we process it only with your consent (your phone’s location permission, and the separate map-help and group-sharing choices), only for the purposes above, and never sell it or use it for targeted advertising.

Legal bases (EU and UK)

We use your data to provide the app and the pass you ask for (contract), with your consent for group sharing and map help (which you can withdraw at any time), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and the maps accurate, using only counts that cannot single you out.

Children

SkiWithMe is not meant for children under 13 (under 16 in the European Union and the United Kingdom) to use on their own. They may use it with a parent or guardian, who should keep group sharing and map help switched off for them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if you think we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Sign-in keys are stored only in hashed form, payments are verified by signed messages from Paddle, and data is kept for the shortest time that works. No system is perfectly secure; we will tell affected users promptly if a breach puts their information at risk.

International transfers

Our providers run servers in many countries, including the United States. Where the law requires it, transfers rely on the providers’ standard contractual safeguards.

Changes

We will post changes here with a new date, and tell you in the app before a change that affects how your location is used takes effect.

Contact
