Ski With Me · icon concepts, round four

Bluebird, drawn more realistically

The twin peaks now have lit and shadowed faces, exposed rock, a far range, soft clouds and snow-dusted pines. Three versions keep the original idea; three add a hint of directions with one extra element each.

Row one

The original idea

Three friends' tracks crossing on the way down.

phone size

1. Tracks in the snow

The tracks are grooves in the snow, as they look from a chairlift. Three small skiers in different jackets finish the lines.

phone size

2. Closer in

Cropped tight on the main peak, so the tracks and skiers are larger. It reads better at phone size and loses some of the scenery.

phone size

3. One colour each

Each friend's track takes the colour of their jacket. A little less natural, but it says three separate people more clearly.

Row two

With a hint of directions

One blue route line or one pin, borrowed from how the app draws directions.

phone size

4. Route to a friend

A blue route runs from your dot on the mountain down to a pin: directions to a friend. The other two tracks stay faint behind it.

phone size

5. Meet here

Three coloured tracks join into one and lead to a meeting pin. It shows the group coming together.

phone size

6. Your line

The same three tracks as the original, with the middle one marked as your route and ending at your location dot. This is the lightest touch of the three.

These are still vector drawings, which is as realistic as a drawing gets without commissioning an illustrator or a 3D render. If you want it to look like a photograph or a painted illustration, the chosen composition here becomes the brief for that.
